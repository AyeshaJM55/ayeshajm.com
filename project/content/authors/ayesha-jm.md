Ayesha J. creates **3D product visualization, photorealistic rendering, CGI animation, and lifestyle imagery** for brands that need clear, flexible visual assets before or after a physical photoshoot is practical.

Her work combines careful modeling, material development, lighting, composition, and commercial art direction. The goal is not simply to make a polished image, but to build a reusable visual system that can support product pages, launches, campaigns, marketplaces, and future variations.

## Areas of focus

- Product modeling and visualization
- Photorealistic studio rendering
- E-commerce image systems
- CGI animation and product films
- Lifestyle scene design and art direction

Every engagement begins by clarifying what the visuals must communicate, where they will be used, and which decisions they need to support.
