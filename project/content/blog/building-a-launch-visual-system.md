A product launch rarely needs one image. It needs a coordinated set of assets that can move between product pages, social posts, presentations, advertising, and marketplace listings.

## Design one visual language

The same product model, material system, lighting direction, and art-direction choices can support several deliverable types:

- studio heroes
- feature close-ups
- lifestyle scenes
- short CGI animation
- vertical and square social crops
- transparent product exports

Treating these as one system improves consistency and reduces duplicated setup work.

## Separate reusable assets from campaign decisions

The product model and approved materials should remain reusable. Campaign-specific environments, props, and motion can then change without rebuilding the product itself.

## Plan crops at the storyboard stage

A widescreen composition does not automatically become a good vertical composition. Important product details, text-safe areas, and camera movement should be checked for every required format before final rendering begins.

The result is a launch package that feels deliberate across platforms instead of a widescreen asset being repeatedly persuaded to fit into smaller rectangles.
